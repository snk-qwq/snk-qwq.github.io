An "Alternative" Proof of Functional Equation of Riemann Zeta Function

Actually it is not really an "alternative" proof, instead of the usual way which I learned about the proof of analytic continuation of a Riemann zeta function, by theta function we choose to seen Riemann Zeta Function as a special case of Heck $L$-function and use the general way to compute analytic continuation of Hecke $L$-function from Tate thesis to apply it to Riemann zeta function.
